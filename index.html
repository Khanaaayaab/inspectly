Questions

Good to know.

Where do I find the VIN?

Look at the base of the windshield on the driver's side, or on the sticker inside the driver's door frame. It is also printed on the title, registration and insurance papers.

Does a report replace an inspection?

No. Reports are informational and do not replace a physical inspection by a qualified professional. Use the findings to decide what to ask a seller or an inspector.

How does payment work?

You pay once for each report. There is no subscription and nothing renews. Payment is handled on a secure checkout page, and we never see or store your card details.

What will my report include?

Coverage depends on the data sources and inspection information available for the vehicle you choose, so the details can differ from one vehicle to another.
